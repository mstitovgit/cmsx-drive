# Privacy Policy — CMSX Drive

CMSX Drive is a personal tool used only by its owner. It signs in to the owner's own Google account and copies the owner's own files into a folder on the owner's own Google Drive, then reads them back only to check that each copy is complete.

- It does not access any other user's data.
- It does not share, sell or send any data to anyone.
- It stores nothing about Google accounts except the sign-in token kept on the owner's own computer.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
